# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This folder contains OctoAcme's project management processes and guidance. OctoAcme takes a customer-first, iterative approach: teams deliver small, testable increments; maintain clear ownership through named project and product leads; use data to inform decisions; and foster psychological safety so people can share feedback and learn.

## Project lifecycle at a glance

1. **Initiation** validates the business need, desired outcomes, stakeholders, and readiness to proceed.
2. **Planning** turns approved work into a prioritized, estimated backlog, with acceptance criteria, milestones, risks, dependencies, and a Definition of Done.
3. **Execution and tracking** moves work through the project board and delivery workflow, including small reviewed pull requests, automated checks and testing, regular status updates, and blocker escalation.
4. **Release and deployment** confirms acceptance criteria and CI/security checks, prepares release notes and rollback plans, deploys, and verifies the change.
5. **Retrospective and continuous improvement** captures what went well and what could improve, then tracks a small set of owned actions and follows up on their impact.
